Superstar Rajinikanth: 50 Years of Cinema Emperor

Shivaji Rao Gaikwad, universally celebrated as Superstar Rajinikanth and Thalaivar, is an Indian cinematic icon who debuted in 1975 under legendary director K. Balachander in Apoorva Raagangal.

From his early days as a Bangalore Transport Service (BTS) Route 10A bus conductor to becoming the undisputed ruler of Indian mass entertainment, his signature swagger, unmatched screen charisma, and blockbuster records span over 170 films across Tamil, Telugu, Hindi, Kannada, and Malayalam cinema.

Frequently Asked Questions About Rajinikanth

What was Rajinikanth's real name and early career?
Rajinikanth was born Shivaji Rao Gaikwad on December 12, 1950, in Bangalore. He worked as a bus conductor for the Bangalore Transport Service (Route 10A) before joining the Madras Film Institute.
How did Rajinikanth get the title Superstar?
He was conferred the title 'Superstar' with his 1978 solo hit Bairavi. The legendary blue-diode title card animation was immortalized in Annaamalai (1992).
What are Rajinikanth's highest grossing movies?
Rajinikanth is the first South Indian actor to deliver four separate ₹500 crore+ grossing films, including Jailer 2 (with an estimated ₹835.80 crore worldwide collection after 38 days), 2.0, Jailer, and Coolie.
What national honours has Rajinikanth received?
Rajinikanth has received India's highest cinematic award, the Dadasaheb Phalke Award (2019), as well as the Padma Vibhushan (2016) and Padma Bhushan (2000).
